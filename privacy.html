Privacy policy

Fradrag · last updated 28 October 2026

Fradrag is a private app with a single user, Alan Lucien Øyen, for the bookkeeping of his own sole proprietorship. This page says what it does with data, including data from Google.

What Fradrag reads from Google

With his permission, Fradrag reads his Gmail with read-only access. It searches for emails that carry receipts, such as a train ticket or a hotel bill, so that he can attach the receipt to the purchase in his accounting. It never sends, changes, labels or deletes email.

Where the data goes

  • Receipts and their details are stored on his own iPhone and Mac and in his own iCloud account.
  • A receipt is sent to his accounting system, Fiken, only when he books it himself.
  • If he chooses to ask about one item, the text of that item may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to answer him. Email is never sent there in bulk.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models by Fradrag.

Google user data

Fradrag's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Signing out in the app also removes the stored sign-in.

Fradrag

A private bookkeeping app for one sole proprietorship.

Fradrag is a small iPhone and Mac app made for Alan Lucien Øyen's own business (org. 925 359 297). It photographs receipts, files them in his accounting system Fiken, and helps him find receipts that arrived by email. It is not offered to the public and has one user.

When he allows it, Fradrag reads his Gmail read-only to find receipt emails. Nothing in the mailbox is changed, sent or deleted.
